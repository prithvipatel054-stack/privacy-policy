Privacy Policy

Hit's Player • Effective and Last Updated: October 2026

In Short: Hit's Player is an offline video and audio player designed to play media already stored on your device. We do not require account registration, we run no backend servers, and your media files never leave your device. The only third-party service involved is Google AdMob, which serves advertisements.

1. Scope of Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Hit's Player ("we", "our", or "the app"), created by developer Hit Zalavadiya, handles information when you install, access, or use our mobile application on Android devices.

By using Hit's Player, you understand and acknowledge that our local player operates entirely offline, while advertising features are provided in collaboration with Google AdMob.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

Unlike cloud streaming services, Hit's Player does not collect personal identifiers or track your media library:

  • No User Accounts: You do not need to sign up, sign in, or provide your name, email address, or phone number.
  • No Media Uploads: Your video and audio files, file paths, and folder titles remain exclusively on your physical device. We never upload or stream your content to any remote servers.
  • No Access to Personal Contacts: We do not access, view, or transfer your contact list, messaging history, or private documents.
  • No Precise Location Tracking: The application does not request, track, or record your GPS or precise geographic coordinates.
  • No Playback Surveillance: We do not operate server infrastructure to track what you watch, listen to, or search for.

3. Third-Party AdMob Data Collection

Hit's Player is a free application supported by advertisements displayed through the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob), including native ads and app open ads.

While Hit's Player itself collects no personal information, Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain technical data for advertising, attribution, and anti-fraud purposes, including:

  • Device Identifiers: Android Advertising ID (AAID) and app-set identifiers.
  • IP Address & Network Data: Used to deliver localized ads (inferring coarse, city-level region) and detect fraud.
  • Hardware & System Info: Device manufacturer, OS version, language setting, and screen size.
  • Ad Interactions: Impressions, clicks, and SDK-level diagnostic crash logs.

Google processes this information in accordance with its privacy terms. For detailed insights, please refer to:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

4. Permissions Explained

Hit's Player asks only for permissions strictly necessary for local playback and ad operations:

Permission Why We Ask If Denied
READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_MEDIA_AUDIO, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE To scan your storage, index local audio and video files into folders, and enable smooth media playback. The app cannot find, display, or play your local media files.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS To show media playback controls (play, pause, next, seek) in your notification shade and lock screen. Media still plays, but controls will not appear in notifications or on the lock screen.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK To keep background audio or video playback running without interruption when the screen is turned off or minimized. Android may immediately kill playback as soon as you exit the app or lock the screen.
WAKE_LOCK Prevents the screen from dimming or sleeping while you are actively watching a video. The device screen may timeout and sleep during video playback.
INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Used exclusively by Google AdMob to load advertising banners. Media playback is 100% offline. Advertisements will not load. Local playback is completely unaffected.

5. Advertising & Consent Management

Depending on your geographic location and preferences, ads may be personalised (tailored to your interests) or non-personalised (contextually targeted). You maintain complete control over your advertising identifier:

  1. Open your Android phone's Settings.
  2. Navigate to Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads).
  3. Tap Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID to opt out of personalized interest profiles.

For users residing in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the UK, consent choices are managed via Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent manager.

6. Local Storage on Device

Hit's Player stores configuration information locally inside your phone's private application sandbox (using SQLite database and SharedPreferences). This includes:

  • Resume timestamps and playback progress for videos and audio tracks
  • Playback settings (speed, loop mode, volume levels, hardware/software decoder preferences)
  • UI layout preferences (dark/light theme, folder sorting)
  • Locally generated thumbnail caches for fast folder listing

How to erase this data: Go to your phone's Settings → Apps → Hit's Player → Storage → tap Clear Data / Clear Storage. Uninstalling the app also automatically removes all app settings and caches. Neither action will ever touch or delete your personal video or music files.

7. Data Security

Because Hit's Player operates offline, your media files and playback configurations are safeguarded by Android's native sandboxing architecture. Third-party ad transmissions conducted by Google AdMob are safeguarded in transit using modern HTTPS/TLS encryption.

8. Children's Privacy

Hit's Player is a general-purpose offline media player and is not specifically designed for or directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. However, third-party services such as Google AdMob may collect device information and advertising-related data as described in this Privacy Policy.

If you believe that a child's personal information has been collected through the app, please contact us at hitsplayer.app@gmail.com so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

9. User Legal Rights

Under international privacy regulations—including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR - EEA & UK), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA - USA), and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act):

  • You have the right to access, rectify, or erase personal data, withdraw advertising consent, and access grievance redressal.
  • Because Hit's Player operates completely offline without user accounts or server databases, we hold no personal identity profiles or viewing records about you.
  • To reset or delete advertising identifiers, use your device's Android settings as described in Section 5. For any privacy requests or grievance redressal, write to our developer email at hitsplayer.app@gmail.com.

10. Data Retention

Playback states and settings remain on your physical device until you clear app data or uninstall the application. Data collected by Google AdMob is retained in accordance with Google's own retention and privacy policies.
